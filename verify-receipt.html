Check a NIS2 board receipt

Every board receipt from the NIS2 gap assessment carries a receipt number. Enter it to see what was issued under that number, and compare it with the document in front of you.

What a match means

  • The receipt was issued, on that date, with that result. If the date, the scores and the answers fingerprint here match the document, the document is the one that was issued. If any of them differ, it is not.
  • It records what was declared, not what was verified. The receipt proves which answers were submitted and when. It is not an audit, a certification or legal advice.
  • Nothing here identifies the buyer. The name on the document is printed from the payment; this page never shows it, nor the individual answers.
